URL encoding (percent-encoding) makes URLs safe for special characters.

Why It's Needed

Characters like spaces, &, ? have special meaning in URLs.

How It Works

Each unsafe character → %XX where XX is its hex code.

Example

"hello world" → "hello%20world".

Encode Instantly

Use a browser-based tool — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

What's the difference between encodeURI and encodeURIComponent?

encodeURI preserves URL structure; encodeURIComponent encodes everything.

Are spaces %20 or +?

%20 in paths, + in query strings (form encoding).
