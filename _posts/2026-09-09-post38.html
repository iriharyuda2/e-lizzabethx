Strong passwords are your first defense against account compromise.

What Makes a Password Strong?

  • Length (16+ characters)
  • Mix of upper, lower, numbers, symbols
  • No personal info
  • Unique per site

Use a Password Manager

It generates and remembers strong passwords for you.

Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds a second layer.

Generate Instantly

Use a browser-based generator — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

How often should I change passwords?

Only after a breach — frequent changes lead to weaker passwords.

Are passphrases better?

Long passphrases can be both strong and memorable.
