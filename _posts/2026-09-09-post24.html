Loan payments are calculated using amortization — each payment covers both interest and principal.

The Formula

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where P = principal, r = monthly rate, n = number of payments.

Example

$10,000 loan at 6% annual for 5 years → monthly payment ≈ $193.33.

Early Payments = More Interest

Early payments go mostly to interest; later payments go mostly to principal.

FAQ

Does a longer loan mean lower payments?

Yes, but you pay more total interest.

Can I pay off a loan early?

Usually yes, but check for prepayment penalties.
