Calculating age sounds simple, but leap years and month lengths make it tricky.

Basic Method

Age = Current year − Birth year, then adjust for whether birthday has passed.

Exact Method

Subtract years, then months, then days — borrowing from the previous unit when needed.

Leap Years

People born on February 29 celebrate on February 28 or March 1 in non-leap years.

FAQ

How do I calculate my age in days?

Subtract birth date from today in milliseconds, then divide by 86,400,000.

Does timezone affect age calculation?

Slightly — but for most purposes it doesn't matter.
