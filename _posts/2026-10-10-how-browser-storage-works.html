Browser storage lets web apps save data locally without a server.

localStorage

Stores key-value pairs persistently — survives page reloads.

sessionStorage

Cleared when the tab closes.

Privacy

Data stays on your device — never sent to any server.

Try It Instantly

Use a browser-based notes tool — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

How much can I store?

Usually 5–10 MB per origin.

Is localStorage secure?

No — don't store sensitive data.
