Percentages are everywhere — discounts, tips, interest rates, test scores. Here are the three formulas you'll use most.

1. What is X% of Y?

Formula: (X ÷ 100) × Y

Example: What is 20% of 150? → (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30.

2. X is what percent of Y?

Formula: (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: 30 is what percent of 150? → (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%.

3. Percentage Change

Formula: ((New − Old) ÷ |Old|) × 100

Example: From 100 to 120 → ((120 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = +20%.

FAQ

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage, then subtract.

What's the difference between percent and percentage point?

A percent is a fraction of 100; a percentage point is the unit of difference between two percentages.
