GPA (Grade Point Average) is a weighted average of your course grades.

The Formula

GPA = Σ(grade × credits) ÷ Σ(credits)

Example

A (4.0, 3 credits), B (3.0, 4 credits), A (4.0, 2 credits) → (12+12+8) ÷ 9 = 3.56.

Weighted vs Unweighted

Weighted GPA gives extra points for honors/AP courses. Unweighted is a simple average.

FAQ

What's a good GPA?

3.5+ is generally considered strong.

Do all schools use the same scale?

No — scales vary (4.0, 5.0, 10.0, etc.).
