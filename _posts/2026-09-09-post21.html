Compound interest is interest on interest. It's the reason savings grow faster over time.

The Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

Where P = principal, r = annual rate, n = compounds per year, t = years.

Example

$1,000 at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years → A ≈ $1,647.

Why Frequency Matters

More frequent compounding = slightly more interest. Daily > monthly > yearly.

FAQ

What's the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is only on principal; compound is on principal + accumulated interest.

How long to double my money?

Use the Rule of 72: 72 ÷ annual rate ≈ years to double.
