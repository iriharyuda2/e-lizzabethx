The Pomodoro Technique uses 25-minute focused work sessions separated by short breaks.

How It Works

  1. Pick a task
  2. Work 25 minutes
  3. Take a 5-minute break
  4. After 4 sessions, take a longer break

Why It Works

Short sessions reduce procrastination and mental fatigue.

Try It Instantly

Use a browser-based timer — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

Can I adjust the 25 minutes?

Yes — some people prefer 50/10.

Does it work for creative work?

Yes — it helps overcome starting friction.
