Accurate timing helps you estimate future tasks and improve productivity.

Start and Stop Cleanly

Begin timing when you actually start, not before.

Avoid Multitasking

Multitasking distorts timing — focus on one task.

Record Results

Keep a log to spot patterns.

Time Instantly

Use a browser-based stopwatch — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

Should I include breaks?

No — time only active work.

How many samples do I need?

At least 3–5 for a reliable estimate.
