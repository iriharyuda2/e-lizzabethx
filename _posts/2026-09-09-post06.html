Lorem Ipsum is dummy text used in design to visualize layout without distracting content.

History

It comes from Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (45 BC), scrambled.

Why Use It?

Real text distracts reviewers. Dummy text lets them focus on design.

Alternatives

Some designers prefer real content or themed dummy text.
