Regular expressions (regex) are patterns for matching text.

Basic Syntax

  • . — any character
  • \d — digit
  • \w — word character
  • * — zero or more
  • + — one or more

Example

\d+ matches one or more digits.

Flags

g (global), i (case-insensitive), m (multiline).

FAQ

Is regex the same in every language?

Mostly, but flavors differ (PCRE, JavaScript, Python).
