Dice are a great way to learn probability.

Single Die

Probability of any number = 1/6 ≈ 16.67%.

Two Dice

Sum of 7 is most likely (6/36 ≈ 16.67%).

Expected Value

Average of all outcomes = (1+2+3+4+5+6)/6 = 3.5.

FAQ

Are dice rolls independent?

Yes — previous rolls don't affect future ones.

What's the gambler's fallacy?

Believing past results affect future random events.
