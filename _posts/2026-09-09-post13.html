Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body burns at rest to maintain vital functions like breathing and circulation.

Why BMR Matters

BMR is the foundation of your daily calorie needs. Knowing it helps you plan meals, lose weight, or maintain weight.

The Mifflin-St Jeor Formula

For men: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + 5

For women: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age − 161

BMR vs TDEE

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) = BMR × activity factor. That's the real number of calories you burn daily.

FAQ

Does BMR change with age?

Yes — BMR typically decreases with age due to muscle loss.

Can I increase BMR?

Building muscle through strength training can help.
