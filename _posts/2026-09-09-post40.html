A Unix timestamp is the number of seconds since January 1, 1970 UTC.

Why 1970?

It was chosen as a convenient epoch for early Unix systems.

Example

1700000000 = November 14, 2023.

Year 2038 Problem

32-bit systems overflow in 2038 — most modern systems use 64-bit.

FAQ

Are timestamps in seconds or milliseconds?

Unix = seconds; JavaScript = milliseconds.

Do timestamps account for timezones?

No — they're always UTC.
