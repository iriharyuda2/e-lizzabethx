Most of the world uses metric; a few countries (like the US) still use imperial.

Common Conversions

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm
  • 1 foot = 0.3048 m
  • 1 mile = 1.609 km
  • 1 pound = 0.4536 kg

Temperature

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

FAQ

Why does the US use imperial?

Historical reasons — the US never fully adopted metric.

Which is more precise?

Metric is generally easier for conversions.
