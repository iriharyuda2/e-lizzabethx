HEX and RGB are two ways to represent the same colors.

HEX

Format: #RRGGBB. Example: #0366d6.

RGB

Format: rgb(r, g, b). Example: rgb(3, 102, 214).

Conversion

Each hex pair converts to a decimal number 0–255.

FAQ

Which is better?

Neither — they're equivalent. HEX is more compact; RGB is easier to adjust.

What about RGBA?

RGBA adds an alpha channel for transparency.
