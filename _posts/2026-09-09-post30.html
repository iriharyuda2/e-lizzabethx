Sorting text is useful for lists, glossaries, and data cleanup.

A–Z vs Z–A

A–Z sorts ascending; Z–A descending.

Case Sensitivity

Uppercase letters usually sort before lowercase in ASCII order.

Numbers

Numbers sort before letters in most systems.

Sort Instantly

Use a browser-based tool — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

Does it remove blank lines?

Yes — empty lines are filtered out.

Can I sort by length?

Not with this tool — only alphabetical.
