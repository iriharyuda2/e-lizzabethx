Tipping customs vary by country, but in many places 15–20% is standard for good service.

Standard Tip Percentages

  • 15% — acceptable service
  • 18% — good service
  • 20%+ — excellent service

Formula

Tip = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100)

Total = Bill + Tip

Per person = Total ÷ Number of people

Example

$80 bill, 18% tip, 4 people → Tip = $14.40, Total = $94.40, Per person = $23.60.

FAQ

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Usually pre-tax, but post-tax is also common.

Is 20% too much?

For good service, 20% is standard in many countries.
