Word count isn't just a number — it affects how readers and search engines perceive your content.

SEO

Longer content (1,000+ words) often ranks higher because it covers topics in depth.

Readability

Very long paragraphs tire readers. Break them up.

Social Media

Twitter/X limits to 280 characters. Instagram captions work best under 125 characters.

Count Instantly

Use a browser-based counter — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

How many words should a blog post be?

1,000–2,000 words is a common target for SEO.

Does character count matter?

Yes — for meta descriptions (150–160 chars) and social posts.
