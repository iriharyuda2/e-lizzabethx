Line numbers make it easy to reference specific parts of a document.

In Coding

Error messages reference line numbers — they help you debug fast.

In Editing

Reviewers can point to "line 42" instead of quoting text.

In Legal Documents

Line numbers make contracts easier to cite.

Add Instantly

Use a browser-based tool — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

Does it preserve formatting?

Yes — each line keeps its content.

Can I remove numbers later?

Yes — reverse manually or use find and replace.
