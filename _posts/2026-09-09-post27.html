Duplicate lines waste space and confuse readers. Here's how to clean them up.

Why Remove Duplicates?

Clean data = accurate analysis, smaller files, better readability.

Methods

  • Manual — slow, error-prone
  • Spreadsheet — sort + remove duplicates
  • Browser tool — instant, private

Sorting vs Deduplicating

Sorting arranges; deduplicating removes repeats. Often both are needed.

Clean Instantly

Use a browser-based tool — no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

Does it remove blank lines?

Yes — empty lines are ignored.

Is it case-sensitive?

Yes — "Hello" and "hello" are different.
