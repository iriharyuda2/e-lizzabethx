Title case capitalizes the first letter of each major word in a title.

Rules

  • Capitalize nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs
  • Lowercase articles (a, an, the), conjunctions (and, or), prepositions (in, on)
  • Always capitalize the first and last word

When to Use

Book titles, article headlines, headings in American English.

When Not to Use

British English often uses sentence case. Choose one and be consistent.

FAQ

Is "to" capitalized in title case?

Usually lowercase, unless it's part of an infinitive verb.

What about "is"?

Capitalize it — it's a verb.
