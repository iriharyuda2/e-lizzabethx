A discount is a reduction from the original price. It's usually expressed as a percentage, sometimes as a fixed amount.

Percentage Discounts

Formula: Final = Original × (1 − Discount ÷ 100)

Example: $80 with 25% off → 80 × (1 − 0.25) = $60.

Stacked Discounts

Two 10% discounts are not 20% off. They're 10% of the reduced price: 100 → 90 → 81 = 19% off.

Fixed Amount Discounts

Simple subtraction: $50 − $10 = $40.

FAQ

Is 20% off the same as 20% cashback?

No — cashback returns money later; discount reduces price now.

How do I calculate sales tax with a discount?

Apply the discount first, then add tax on the final price.
