Image compression reduces file size while keeping acceptable quality.

Lossy vs Lossless

Lossy (JPEG) discards some data; lossless (PNG) preserves all.

When to Compress

Always — smaller images = faster websites, lower bandwidth.

Quality Settings

70–85% is usually a good balance.

Compress Instantly

Use a browser-based tool — no upload, no data sent anywhere.

FAQ

Does compression reduce quality?

Lossy compression does — but often imperceptibly.

What's the best format?

WebP for web, JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics.
