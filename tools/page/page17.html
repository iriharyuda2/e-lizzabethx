What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index estimates body fat based on height and weight. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

BMI Categories

  • Under 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5–24.9 — Normal
  • 25–29.9 — Overweight
  • 30+ — Obese

FAQ

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

No. Muscle mass can raise BMI despite low body fat.
